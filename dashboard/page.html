    Definitions and what this leaves out
    Version
    A build of Ranmaru. Bots on the same build share a row.
    Win rate
    Wins out of games played, on panelattack.com. Fights are not counted.
    Plausible range
    95% Wilson interval. Overlapping lines can't be told apart yet.
    Opponent topped out
    Share of the game the opponent was in the top row.
    Missing
    p30 and the unnumbered run saved no log, so they are not counted.
    Source
    Each run's saved bot log. Updates when a set ends while a bot runs.
